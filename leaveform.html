Instructions and Requirements

Applications for any type of leave must be accomplished on this form in at least two (2) copies and submitted together with the corresponding supporting documents outlined below:

1. Vacation Leave

File at least five (5) working days ahead of the intended leave date whenever possible. Indicate whether the leave will be spent within the Philippines or abroad to facilitate travel authority and clearance requirements.

2. Mandatory / Forced Leave

The required five-day annual vacation leave is forfeited if unused within the calendar year, unless cancelled by the agency head due to service exigency. Any approved vacation leave of one (1) day or more counts toward this requirement under CSC rules.

3. Sick Leave*

Submit immediately upon returning to work. Applications filed in advance or covering more than five (5) consecutive days require a medical certificate (or a sworn affidavit if no medical consultation took place).

4. Maternity Leave* – 105 days

  • Medical proof of pregnancy (e.g., ultrasound or physician's certification indicating expected delivery date).
  • Accomplished Notice of Allocation of Maternity Leave Credits (CS Form No. 6a), when applicable.
  • Seconded female personnel receive full pay from the receiving agency.

5. Paternity Leave – 7 days

Attach proof of child delivery (such as a birth certificate or medical certificate) together with a copy of the marriage contract.

6. Special Privilege Leave – 3 days

File for approval at least one (1) week prior to availment, except during emergencies. Specify local or foreign destination for travel authority and clearance purposes.

7. Solo Parent Leave – 7 days

Submit in advance—ideally five (5) working days before the leave—along with a valid, updated Solo Parent Identification Card.

8. Study Leave* – up to 6 months

  • Compliance with internal agency guidelines and qualification rules.
  • Executed service contract between the employee and the agency head or authorized representative.

9. 10-Day VAWC Leave

May be filed before or immediately after the leave period, supported by any of the following:

  • Barangay Protection Order (BPO) or court-issued Temporary/Permanent Protection Order (TPO/PPO);
  • Certification from the Punong Barangay, Kagawad, Prosecutor, or Clerk of Court confirming a pending protection order application; or
  • Police report detailing the incident and a medical certificate, subject to the immediate supervisor's evaluation.

10. Rehabilitation Privilege* – up to 6 months

  • Submit within one (1) week from the date of the accident unless extended for valid reasons.
  • Written letter request with relevant incident or police reports.
  • Medical certificate detailing injuries, treatment plan, and required recovery period (plus written concurrence from a government physician if treated by a private doctor).

11. Special Leave Benefits for Women* – up to 2 months

File at least five (5) days before scheduled gynecological surgery (or immediately upon return in emergency cases, provided the agency was notified during confinement). Attach a medical certificate and clinical summary from the attending surgeon detailing the diagnosis, surgical procedure, operative technique, confinement period, and estimated recovery duration.

12. Special Emergency (Calamity) Leave – up to 5 days

Granted once per year for up to five (5) straight or staggered working days within thirty (30) days from the calamity occurrence, subject to agency verification of the employee's residence within a declared calamity area.

13. Monetization of Leave Credits

Requests to monetize 50% or more of accumulated leave credits must include a formal letter request to the agency head stating valid and justifiable reasons.

14. Terminal Leave*

Attach official proof of resignation, retirement, or separation from government service.

15. Adoption Leave

Include an authenticated copy of the Pre-Adoptive Placement Authority issued by the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD).

* For leave of absence of thirty (30) calendar days or more and terminal leave, application shall be accompanied by a clearance from money, property, and work-related accountabilities (pursuant to CSC Memorandum Circular No. 2, s. 1985).
